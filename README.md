Entry for the [make.fun bounty "One-Liner Laughs"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-404-one-liner-laughs-aa872d41): a single funny line of at most 200 characters, judged pairwise by an AI judge on whether it is a real one-line joke, how funny it is, the surprise of its turn, its freshness and its economy of wording.

## The entry

```
I asked my AI assistant for motivation; it booked a meeting between me and my excuses — they hit it off.
```

104 characters. The joke: asking an AI for motivation sets up a productivity answer, but the assistant books a meeting with my excuses instead — and we get along. The twist is the point, and the meeting-booking detail is specific to how AI assistants actually behave.

## How it works

`main.ts` is a deterministic, standard-library-only TypeScript program. It writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and validates it against the competition gate first (UTF-8 text, 1 to 200 characters, not only white space).

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
